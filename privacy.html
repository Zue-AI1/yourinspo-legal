Your Inspo

Privacy Policy · Effective 13 November 2026

Who we are

Your Inspo ("the app", "we", "us") is an inspirational quotes and Bible verse app for Android. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we store your email address and a user ID through Google Firebase Authentication. If you use the app as a guest, an anonymous user ID is created.
  • Free trial record. To make sure the 7-day free trial is used only once per device, we store a device identifier (Android ID, which is specific to this app on your device), your user ID and the trial start and end times.
  • Your content. Favorite quotes, topics and sources, personal quotes you write, and your latest personalized quote. These are stored on your device and, if you have an account, in your private area of our database so they can be restored when you log in.
  • Searches. The topics, authors or sources you search for are sent to our server to find a quote. Search results may be cached anonymously to reduce repeated requests. Cached results are not linked to you.
  • Subscription information. If you subscribe, Google Play handles the payment. We receive and store the subscription status, product and purchase token so we can confirm your premium access. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • Reminder settings. Your reminder days, times and sound or vibration choice are stored only on your device.
  • Advertising. If ads are shown to free users, Google AdMob may collect the advertising ID and device information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone data.

How we use information

  • To provide the daily quote, personalized search, Bible features, favorites and reminders.
  • To manage your account, free trial and subscription.
  • To keep the service reliable and prevent abuse of the free trial.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (authentication, database and cloud functions), Google Play Billing (subscriptions), Google AdMob (ads for free users) and the They Said So® quotes service (quote search; only the search text is sent, never your identity).

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, favorites, personal quotes and synced data. The free-trial device record is kept to prevent repeat trials. See how to delete your account.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that only you can read your own account data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
