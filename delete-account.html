Delete from the app

  1. Open Your Inspo and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If asked, enter your password to verify it's you.

This permanently deletes your account, email address, favorite quotes, topics and sources, personal quotes and synced data. It cannot be undone.

What is kept

To prevent repeated free trials, a record of the device identifier and trial dates is kept. Subscription payment records are kept by Google Play under its own policies.

Active subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Can't access the app?

Email YourInspo.Support@gmail.com from the email you registered with and ask for your account to be deleted. We'll complete the request within 30 days.
